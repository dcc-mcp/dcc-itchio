# Acquisition and license policy

- Browse only the official `itch.io/game-assets...xml` RSS surface.
- Authenticated operations use a fresh temporary `butlerd` database and API-key RPC login.
- List results are restricted to `classification=assets` and owned download keys.
- Version 0.1 accepts direct owned download keys only; bundle-only ownership is out of scope.
- Inspection and download require the exact `download_key_id` and `game_id` pair.
- Download additionally requires an exact `upload_id`, explicit license text, and an HTTPS license source.
- Ownership is access evidence, not license evidence.
- The provider never executes downloaded content or imports it into a DCC.
- Rejected downloaded content remains quarantined for deliberate review or deletion.
